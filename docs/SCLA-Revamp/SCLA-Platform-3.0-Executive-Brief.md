# SCLA Platform 3.0: From Holter Monitoring to Comprehensive Cardiac Care

## Executive Summary

SCLA Platform 3.0 transforms our existing Holter monitoring app into a comprehensive cardiac health platform by adding continuous health tracking and structured improvement programs. 

**The Big Picture**: We keep users engaged through daily health modules while providing always-on monitoring. Think of it as "Duolingo for heart health" - bite-sized daily tasks that improve cardiac outcomes.

**Key Innovation**: Time-bound health modules (14-30 days) that transform boring data collection into engaging health journeys with clear outcomes.

**Business Impact**: 
- Expand from one-time Holter studies to continuous subscription model
- Increase user retention from days to months
- Create new revenue streams through module subscriptions
- Build defensible moat through comprehensive health data

---

## The Concept: Two Layers of Engagement

### Layer 1: Basic Monitoring (Foundation)
Always-on health tracking that runs in the background:
- 24/7 ECG monitoring via SKIIN device
- Sleep quality and heart rate patterns
- Activity and exercise tracking
- Blood pressure and weight logging
- Symptom diary with ECG correlation

### Layer 2: Health Modules (Engagement Driver)
Time-bound programs that give users a reason to engage daily:

**1. Heart Health Screening (14 days)**
- For: Annual checkups, new cardiac patients
- Outcome: Comprehensive screening report for doctors
- Tasks: Daily vitals, symptom logging, activity tracking

**2. AFib Management (30 days)**
- For: AFib patients seeking trigger identification
- Outcome: Personal trigger map and burden trends
- Tasks: Episode logging, trigger diary, pattern tracking

**3. Blood Pressure Control (21 days)**
- For: Hypertension patients optimizing treatment
- Outcome: Medication timing and lifestyle insights
- Tasks: AM/PM readings, medication tracking, exercise correlation

**4. Cardiac Fitness (28 days)**
- For: Building safe exercise habits post-event
- Outcome: Personalized exercise prescription
- Tasks: Heart zone training, recovery monitoring, progress tracking

## Appendix: Module Deep Dives

### Heart Health Screening Module
**Clinical Protocol**: Modified AHA preventive screening guidelines
**Daily Time**: 5 minutes morning, 2 minutes evening
**Key Measurements**: Resting ECG, orthostatic vitals, symptom inventory
**Provider Value**: Replaces manual screening with automated report

### AFib Management Module  
**Clinical Protocol**: Based on ACC/AHA AFib guidelines
**Daily Time**: 2-3 minutes throughout day
**Key Features**: Trigger correlation, burden trending, episode classification
**Provider Value**: Objective trigger identification for treatment planning

### Blood Pressure Optimization Module
**Clinical Protocol**: JNC8 hypertension management guidelines
**Daily Time**: 3 minutes morning, 3 minutes evening
**Key Features**: Medication timing experiments, lifestyle correlation
**Provider Value**: Data-driven medication adjustments

### Cardiac Fitness Module
**Clinical Protocol**: ACSM cardiac rehabilitation guidelines
**Daily Time**: During exercise + 2 minutes logging
**Key Features**: HR zone training, recovery monitoring, safety alerts
**Provider Value**: Objective exercise prescription validation
